# The big picture — what this engine does

The whole factory in one page. Each step below has its own run-sheet, format standards and validators
(see [INDEX.md](INDEX.md)); this is the thirty-second version.

## 1 · Establish the ground truth

Transcribe the official units of competency, verbatim, and consolidate them into one tagged document
per cluster.

- Every PC / PE / KE / FS item gets a canonical tag — the currency every later artefact trades in.
- Validators prove the transcription is verbatim and the consolidation covers every item exactly once.

## 2 · Plan and build the assessments

From the consolidated units, plan the assessments, then author the instruments.

- The plan proves every unit requirement is covered by some assessment task before anything is authored.
- Instruments are guided workbooks: numbered tasks the student works through, generated from one
  source that renders the student copy, the assessor copy and the marking machinery.
- The scenario (the in-world client and its website) is built from the assessment plan's requirements —
  the world exists to make the assessments doable.

## 3 · Mirror the assessments as practice

Each assessment workbook gets a practice twin: same structure, same task shapes, different specifics
and context, plus step-by-step guidance.

- Students rehearse exactly what the assessment asks, without being given the assessment.
- The practice sheet is the learning tool; its task numbers are what the teaching points at.

## 4 · Build the teaching

Plan and build the slide decks, one topic per continuous run of practice-workbook tasks.

- Two obligations, both machine-gated: every unit-of-competency requirement is mappable to a slide
  ("where is PC 2.3 covered? — this slide here"), and every activity slide points at numbered
  practice-workbook tasks ("do tasks 5 to 9 now").
- The class works through a deck, hits an activity, and does the next tasks on their worksheet — the
  deck and the workbook advance together.

## 5 · Deliver

Fit the built topics to a specific intake: the delivery plan places every topic and assessment into
the semester's actual sessions.

- Per-intake and disposable; everything upstream of it is reusable machinery.
